A little inspiration

A vacation retreat for family, friends, and unforgettable memories.

Located in Graham, North Carolina, Graham Gem sits smack-dab between Greensboro and Durham so your group can enjoy the best of both cities, nearby universities, local dining, parks, gardens, and easy day trips.

This spacious 5-bedroom, 2.5-bath home is made for gathering together — with a full kitchen, private seasonal pool, fenced backyard, arcade table, outdoor dining space, and fire pit for celebrating, reconnecting, and making the kind of memories people talk about long after the trip.
